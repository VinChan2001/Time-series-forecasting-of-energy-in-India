# **Time-Series Forecasting of Energy in India** ⚡📈  

## **Overview**  
This project focuses on **time-series forecasting** for **energy consumption and production trends in India**. By leveraging **machine learning models and statistical techniques**, it aims to provide **accurate energy demand predictions**, helping policymakers and businesses make informed decisions.  

## **Objectives**  
- Analyze historical **energy consumption and production** data in India.  
- Build **time-series forecasting models** to predict future trends.  
- Develop **visualizations and dashboards** for insights.  
- Evaluate model performance using statistical metrics.  

## **Technologies Used**  
- **Python** (Pandas, NumPy, Scikit-learn, TensorFlow)  
- **Time-Series Forecasting Models** (ARIMA, SARIMA, LSTM)  
- **SQL** (for data extraction and transformation)  
- **Power BI / Tableau** (for interactive visualizations)  
- **Jupyter Notebook**  

## **Dataset**  
The dataset includes **historical energy production and consumption data** from various sources, such as:  
- Government energy reports  
- Renewable and non-renewable energy sources  
- Seasonal and yearly energy demand variations  
- External factors affecting energy trends  

## **Key Features**  
✔ **Data Cleaning & Preprocessing** – Handling missing values and transforming raw data.  
✔ **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)** – Identifying trends, seasonality, and anomalies.  
✔ **Time-Series Forecasting Models** – Implementing ARIMA, SARIMA, and LSTM models.  
✔ **Evaluation Metrics** – Measuring accuracy using RMSE, MAE, and MAPE.  
✔ **Interactive Dashboards** – Visualizing energy trends and forecasts.
